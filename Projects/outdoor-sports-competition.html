Outdoor Sports Competition App

A mobile app designed for people who enjoy outdoor activities like fishing, hunting, and shooting. The focus was to create a space where users can explore events, keep track of their catches, and stay connected with a community that shares similar interests.

Research & User Insights

  • User Research:
    Looked into online communities, forums, and existing apps to understand how users find events and track their activities.
  • Target Audience:
    Outdoor enthusiasts who enjoy activities like fishing and hunting, along with event organizers.
  • User Personas:
    Regular outdoor hobbyists who like tracking their catches and staying updated with local events.

The app is designed to make it easy to explore nearby events, log catches without much effort, and have everything in one place. Location-based features help users quickly see what’s happening around them without needing to jump between different platforms.

Problem Statement

  • Lack of centralized platform for outdoor sports competitions
  • Hard to find reliable and up-to-date information about local events
  • Catch tracking is often inconsistent or completely manual

Final Outcome & Impact

  • Improved User Experience:
    Users can now find relevant events and manage their activity in one place, reducing the effort spent searching across multiple sources.
  • Stronger Community Engagement:
    By allowing users to share their catches and stay updated, the app creates a more connected and active outdoor community.
