Mobile Massage Website

A mobile-first booking platform designed for a mobile massage service where users can easily explore treatments, check availability, and book professional massage sessions at home, hotels, or workplaces. The goal was to create a smooth, calming experience that reflects relaxation and trust from the very first interaction.

Research & User Insights

  • User Research:
    Studied how users typically book mobile massage services online and analyzed existing service websites to understand common friction points, such as complex booking flows and lack of clear service information
  • Target Audience:
    Busy professionals and individuals looking for convenient at-home wellness services without visiting a spa or clinic
  • User Personas:
    Users who value comfort and time-saving solutions, preferring to book quick relaxation or therapy sessions directly from their phone

The platform focuses on making booking feel effortless and reassuring. Users can quickly browse services, understand treatment options, and schedule appointments without unnecessary steps or confusion.

Problem Statement

  • Booking mobile massage services often feels unclear and unstructured
  • Users struggle to quickly understand available treatments and pricing
  • Lack of a smooth, mobile-friendly booking experience

Final Outcome & Impact

  • Simplified Booking Experience:
    Users can easily explore services and book appointments in just a few steps
  • Improved User Confidence:
    Clear service presentation and structured flow help users feel more comfortable and assured when booking
