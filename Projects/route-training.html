Route Training & Mapping App

A mobile platform designed for drivers who follow fixed or semi-fixed routes. Initially built for the waste collection industry, the app helps companies record routes and make it easier for new drivers to learn and follow them without confusion.

Research & User Insights

  • User Research:
    Reviewed how companies currently train drivers and manage routes through internal tools, notes, and manual guidance methods
  • Target Audience:
    Fleet-based businesses with recurring routes, especially industries like waste collection and logistics
  • User Personas:
    New drivers who need clear guidance on routes, and experienced drivers or managers who handle route planning and training

The app focuses on simplifying route learning by providing clear, guided navigation along pre-recorded paths. It reduces the dependency on verbal instructions and makes onboarding new drivers more structured and consistent.

Problem Statement

  • Training new drivers takes time and often depends on manual guidance
  • Route details are not properly documented or shared
  • High chances of confusion or mistakes when following unfamiliar routes

Final Outcome & Impact

  • Improved Training Efficiency:
    New drivers can understand and follow routes more quickly with guided support
  • Reduced Operational Errors:
    Clear route mapping helps minimize mistakes and improves overall consistency in daily operations
